## 2025-02-23 - Extreme Math Loop Optimization in V8
**Learning:** In tight inner loops executing hundreds of thousands of times (like 3D Perlin noise), context lookups (`this.property`) and frequent small function calls (`fade`, `lerp`, `grad`) incur noticeable V8 execution overhead. While V8 is good at inlining, explicitly hoisting static arrays out of classes/objects to module-scoped constants (`const P = ...`) and manually inlining math operations paired with pre-calculated `Float64Array` lookup tables drastically outperforms native branching and bitwise operations.
**Action:** When optimizing hot math loops that fail performance budgets, extract constants to module scope to eliminate `this` context, and manually inline mathematical interpolation functions while replacing heavy branching logic with pre-allocated typed array lookups.
