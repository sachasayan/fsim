## 2024-05-18 - Optimizing Perlin Noise Generation in Hot Loops
**Learning:** Extracting methods like `lerp` and `grad` out of the object into module-scoped functions, pulling static arrays like `permutation` out, and manually inlining simple calls like `fade` within `Noise.noise` avoids object context lookup overhead (`this.*`) and significantly reduces runtime from ~153ms to ~106ms. Replacing Math.floor with bitwise operators caused either breaking behaviour or negligible speedups that weren't worth the loss of readability in this specific iteration.
**Action:** Always consider module scoping and basic manual inlining for math functions in extremely hot loops (like Perlin noise called millions of times) before resorting to obscure bitwise math. Keep optimizations readable where possible.
