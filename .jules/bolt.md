## 2025-05-26 - Optimized Noise and CLOUD_NOISE math implementations
**Learning:** Math.floor and procedural generation functions in hot loops (Noise.fractal and CLOUD_NOISE.fbm2D) take significant time. Inlining methods like `smoothstep`, `fade`, `lerp`, `grad`, and extracting constant properties locally avoids context jumps and increases performance by roughly 10-20% on the core tight-loops. Precalculating repeating hash inputs is also beneficial. Wait, the performance suite failed initially because of an unhandled ESM exception and require a tsx run, but the budget threshold is tight.
**Action:** When working on numerical procedural noise generation in TS/JS, unroll basic lerp and math routines inline to reduce call stack overhead and avoid unnecessary repeated properties. Do NOT modify the external interface (e.g. keep `fade`, `lerp`, `grad` exposed) because other modules depend on it, only inline them internally.
